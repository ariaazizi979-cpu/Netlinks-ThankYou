Dear NetLinks,

Dear every team, teacher, mentor, volunteer, intern and friend who made this place possible,

There are moments when the heart is so full that words feel too small. This is one of those moments.

NetLinks was not a place we built ourselves. It was a gift. When so many doors were closed to us, you opened one, and behind it we found light, a seat to learn in, and a reason to believe in tomorrow.

Every lesson you taught, every patient answer, every late hour and every gentle smile still lives inside us. You did not only teach us to read and to learn. You taught us that we matter, and that our dreams are worth fighting for.

To UNICEF and to every supporter who stood beside us when it mattered most: because of you, we are not just students. We are daughters with hope, sisters with courage, and women who will one day lift others the way you lifted us.

We promise to carry what you gave us wherever we go, and to pass the light on. You will live in our hearts forever.

With our love and endless thanks,
Arya Azizi
